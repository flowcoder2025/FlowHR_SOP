## 5. 데이터 모델 매핑 (04-data-model.md)

- `legal_documents` (LegalDocument): id, type (terms|privacy), version (semver), language (ko|en), effective_date, content_md, created_at
- `(type, version, language)` UNIQUE — ko/en 동시 게시 의무
- `user_consents` (UserConsent): user_id, document_id, version, consented_at, ip, user_agent
